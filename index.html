Support

Quick answers, and a real person if you need one.

I bought Remove Ads on a new phone. How do I get it back?

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore on the Remove Ads card. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Yes. Your levels, stars and Daily Knot streak are saved only on your phone. There are no accounts, so deleting the app deletes them.

I'm stuck on a level.

Tap Hint to light up a rope that's free. You get 3 free hints every day.

Can I change how ads use my data?

Yes. In the EU, UK and Switzerland, open Settings and tap Privacy choices. Everywhere else, use your phone's ad settings. Details are in the privacy policy.
