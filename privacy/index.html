Knotty Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Short version: Knotty has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads.

Who we are

Knotty is a puzzle game made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the Knotty app on iOS and Android.

Data stored on your device

Knotty saves the following only on your device. We never receive it:

  • your level, stars and Daily Knot results and streaks;
  • hints and hearts used;
  • your settings (sound, haptics, colorblind mode, language);
  • whether you bought Remove Ads.

Deleting the app deletes all of this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Knotty shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID on Android or the identifier for vendors on iOS;
  • your IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • information about ad interactions and app usage;
  • diagnostic and performance data.

Google processes this data under its own policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Knotty asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ad loads, and you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy choices. Everywhere, you can also limit ad personalization in your phone's settings: on Android, open Settings → Google → Ads; on iOS, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Buying Remove Ads stops interstitial ads between levels. Optional rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for a hint or an extra heart, may still be offered.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation that Remove Ads was bought, so it can unlock it.

Children

Knotty is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app hands a short text summary of your result to your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data collected by Google for advertising, use the controls above or contact Google. If you have questions, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
